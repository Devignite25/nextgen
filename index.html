Veteran Programs · Free to Veterans

Veterans: Two Ways Into Farming

You served. Now let us help you farm.

NextGen AgriSolutions runs two USDA-funded programs for military veterans in North Florida. One is a full training program on a working farm. The other helps you, wherever you are starting from, get your USDA farm record, your first application, and your first sale. Both are free to veterans. You can sign up for one or both with the same form.

Prefer to talk? Call 904-239-7286 or email veterans@nextgenagrisolutions.org.
Not a veteran but know one? Send them this page.

Veteran Agricultural Pathways

The hands-on training program

Who it's for: Veterans of any era who want a career in agriculture — your own farm, a job on one, or an agribusiness. No farming experience required. Service-disabled veterans are encouraged to apply; at least a quarter of each class is reserved for them.

What you get:

  • A year-long program with about 20 other veterans
  • 120+ hours of classroom and live online instruction, plus 200 hours of hands-on work at a 168-acre working farm in Jackson County
  • Four multi-day residential blocks timed to the crop season: planning and planting, growing, harvest, and processing through to delivery
  • Lodging at the farm's training lodge, meals during residential blocks, and travel support
  • Personal connections to USDA's Farm Service Agency, NRCS, Rural Development, Extension, the SBA Veterans Business Outreach Center, DOL-VETS, and the VA
  • A written pathway plan, a mentor, and placement help

When: The first class begins in early 2027. Applications are open now; space is limited to 20 per year.
Cost: None. No tuition, no fee.

From Service to Soil

Outreach and USDA program help

Who it's for: Veterans in North Florida who are farming on any scale, who have land and want to start, or who are thinking about it. Under USDA's definition, that includes veterans who have never farmed, who have farmed ten years or less, or who left service within the last ten years.

What you get:

  • A veteran peer navigator — an experienced veteran producer who meets with you, helps you figure out what you need, and goes with you to the USDA Service Center and stays until your application is decided
  • Farm Record Days, where FSA staff are on site to set up your USDA customer record and farm number in one visit
  • Service Center Circuit stops near you, hosted by County Veteran Service Officers and veteran organizations, where FSA, NRCS, and Rural Development staff come to you
  • Short training series on farm start-up, recordkeeping, production, food safety, and selling to institutional buyers
  • One-on-one help with land, financing, and first sales, mentoring, and a buyer for your first crop through our partner cooperative's aggregation channel

Where: Two hubs — Northeast Florida (Clay, Duval, Nassau, Baker, and St. Johns Counties) and the Panhandle (Jackson, Holmes, Washington, Calhoun, Gadsden, and Bay Counties).
When: Navigators begin taking cases this winter; the first Farm Record Days and Circuit stops are scheduled for early 2027. Sign up now and we'll contact you when a stop is scheduled near you.
Cost: None.

Veteran service organizations, County Veteran Service Officers, and churches that would like to host a Circuit stop: email Karly@nextgenagrisolutions.org.

USDA Awards NextGen AgriSolutions Two Grants to Put North Florida Veterans on the Land

Orange Park, Fla. — October 2026

NextGen AgriSolutions Inc. has been awarded two three-year grants from the U.S. Department of Agriculture's National Institute of Food and Agriculture, totaling $1.5 million, to help military veterans in North Florida start, grow, and sustain farms.

The first award, under USDA's Enhancing Agricultural Opportunities for Military Veterans (AgVets) program, funds Veteran Agricultural Pathways, a hands-on training program at a working farm in Jackson County. Each year, a cohort of 20 veterans will complete more than 300 hours of classroom and field training that follows a crop from planting through harvest, processing, and delivery to institutional buyers, with residential training blocks at the farm's veteran-designed lodge, a mentor, an individual pathway plan, and placement support into farm employment, a farm of their own, an apprenticeship, or continued agricultural education.

The second award, under USDA's Outreach and Assistance for Veteran Farmers and Ranchers (2501) program, funds From Service to Soil, which brings USDA's farm programs to veterans where they already gather. Veteran peer navigators, experienced veteran producers paid to walk other veterans through the process, will accompany veterans into USDA Service Centers; Farm Record Days with Farm Service Agency staff on site will establish each veteran's USDA customer record and farm number, the gateway to every USDA loan and conservation program; and a Service Center Circuit will bring FSA, NRCS, and Rural Development staff to stops hosted by County Veteran Service Officers and veteran organizations from the Panhandle to Jacksonville.

“Veterans tell us the same thing every year: they want to farm, and nobody showed them the door. These two programs are the door. One teaches you to farm; the other walks you into the Service Center and stays until the paperwork is done.”
— Karly McGraw, Founder and Executive Director

Both programs are delivered in coalition with the New North Florida Cooperatives Association, a veteran-led producer cooperative that has served farmers in the Florida Panhandle for three decades, and are independently evaluated by the American Farm to Institute. Over three years the two projects expect to reach more than 900 veterans, train 60 in the cohort program, establish 240 USDA customer records and 150 farm numbers, and help 60 veteran farms get started or grow.

Veterans interested in either program can sign up on the Veteran Programs page. Program partners, host sites, and employers can contact NextGen at Karly@nextgenagrisolutions.org.

NextGen AgriSolutions Inc. is a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Orange Park that connects veterans and small producers to agricultural training, markets, and USDA programs.
